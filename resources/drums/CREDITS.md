# Drum credits

## Kit

The FLAC files and `kit.xml` here are a stereo mixdown of **DRSKit** by Lars Muldjord and Bent Bisballe Nyeng, recorded on a kit by Jes Eiler of DRSDrums. Drum samples provided by [DrumGizmo.org](https://www.drumgizmo.org/wiki/doku.php?id=kits:drskit). The source was the DrumGizmo download, https://www.drumgizmo.org/kits/DRSKit/DRSKit.zip, not the SFZ repackaging on GitHub.

DRSKit is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The 2013 `README.TXT` inside the kit's download still names CC BY-NC 3.0, so this was checked with the authors: Bent Bisballe Nyeng confirmed by email on 2026-11-03 that CC BY 4.0 is the correct license.

Changes made for RoughTrack with `roughtrack_bench build-kit`: each drum's close microphones, overheads and room microphones were mixed to stereo, the microphones aimed at other drums were left out, a subset of hits was kept, each hit was trimmed and faded, levels were set, and the audio was converted to 48 kHz.

When these samples are used in a composition, any context in which other credits are presented, such as to performers, must include an attribution.

## Grooves

`grooves.xml` is cut from the **Groove MIDI Dataset** v1.0.0 by Jon Gillick, Adam Roberts, Peter Rob and David Bansal, Google Magenta, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes made for RoughTrack with `tools/build_grooves.py`: performances were aligned to their bars, steady 8-bar sections and whole-bar fills were selected, and notes were reduced to thirteen drum sounds.
